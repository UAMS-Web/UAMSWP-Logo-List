UAMSWP Logo List adds a **UAMS Logo List** block to WordPress: a section that shows the logos of partner organizations, chosen by category and label, as a grid or a vertical list. Editors keep each organization as a **Logo List Item** and place the block on any page.

This wiki holds the documentation: a walkthrough, task guides, the reference and troubleshooting. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/UAMSWP-Logo-List#readme) keeps the overview, the development notes and the credits.
